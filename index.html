A simple place to begin

Don't dismiss.
Get curious.

Children notice more than we sometimes realise. What happens in their bodies. Their emotions. How people and places feel.

And sometimes they notice things they cannot easily explain.

We don't always need to have the answer. Sometimes the most powerful thing we can say is:

“Tell me more.”

The Authentic Kids approach

Children don't need to be fixed.
They need to be understood.

The work begins with awareness and trust in their own internal experience: what they feel, what they notice, and how they make sense of the world around them.

Intuition, energy & wonder

There's more to childhood
than meets the eye.

Some children have strong gut feelings, vivid dreams, a deep sensitivity to people or places, or experiences they struggle to put into words.

At Authentic Kids, we make space to explore intuition, energy and our inner world through imagination, movement, playful experiments and curiosity.

We don't need to decide what every experience means. We can notice it, explore it, ask questions and discover more together.

Families bring different beliefs. Personal experiences, imagination and spiritual interpretations can be explored without treating them as scientific proof. Curiosity and self-trust can grow alongside discernment and practical safety.

For the parent who notices

Maybe you've noticed
something too.

Perhaps your child is incredibly sensitive. Maybe they feel everything deeply, struggle to say no, worry about other people's feelings or seem to notice things other children don't.

Perhaps their confidence has changed. Maybe their spark doesn't seem quite as bright. Or perhaps nothing is “wrong” at all. You simply want to protect the connection your child already has with themselves.

You don't need to arrive with all the answers.
We can get curious together.

The person behind Authentic Kids

Hi, I'm Steph.

I'm Steph Ashyggton, a Children's Wellbeing Consultant and founder of Authentic Kids.

My work brings together my professional background as a former Specialist Public Health Nurse, my experience as a home-educating mother of four boys, and my lifelong curiosity about intuition, sensitivity and the things we sometimes experience but cannot easily explain.

I believe children come into the world deeply connected: to their bodies, their feelings, their instincts, the people around them and, for some families, something greater.

My work isn't about telling children what to believe. It's about giving them the space to explore who they are.
