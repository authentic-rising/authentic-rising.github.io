The invitation

Don't dismiss.
Get curious.

Sometimes our children feel things deeply. Sometimes they struggle to explain what is happening inside.

This masterclass is an invitation to pause and explore a more curious response: listening to what children feel and notice, while supporting their boundaries, discernment and practical safety.

Join Steph live on Zoom, with access to the recording afterwards so you can return to it in your own time.

Suggested themes · For review

Making room for
their inner world.

A starting point for the class outline. Steph will confirm the final topics.

  1. Understanding feelings

    Getting curious about emotions and the signals children notice in their bodies.

  2. Listening with curiosity

    Opening up conversations without needing to provide every answer.

  3. Supporting their voice

    Making space for needs, boundaries and the confidence to say no.

Your host

Hi, I'm Steph.

Founder of Authentic Kids, Children's Wellbeing Consultant and mother of four boys.

My work brings together my background as a former Specialist Public Health Nurse, my experience as a home-educating mother and my curiosity about how children experience the world.

I want to help children understand what they feel, develop their voice and stay connected to themselves.
