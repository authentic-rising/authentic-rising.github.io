Before Baby Arrives, Let's Talk About You.

Because birth is one day. Parenting is a lifetime.

An online masterclass for expectant mums and dads to explore your own emotions, the experiences you will bring into parenting and how these can influence your relationships within your family.

Save My Place 💜

You're preparing for a baby. But who's preparing you?

There's so much to think about when you're expecting a baby. Most things are centred on the birth experience and the few weeks after.

But amongst all that preparation, there's something we don't always make time to explore.

Ourselves.

Is this for you?

Have you thought about how you'll make space for each other while learning to care for someone new?

Are you worried about getting parenting wrong, even if you haven't started yet?

Have you ever wondered how you'll manage your own emotions when you're tired, frustrated or things don't go to plan?

If even one of these thoughts has crossed your mind, let's get curious about it together.

Are you expecting a baby and curious about the kind of parent you want to be?

Perhaps you've started thinking about your own childhood, the things you'd like to carry forward and the things you might want to do differently.
Or maybe you've been so busy preparing for your baby's arrival that you haven't really had a chance to think about yourself in all of this.

Whether it's your first baby or your fourth, this is an opportunity to pause, reflect and explore a few things you might not have considered before.

You don't need any previous knowledge, and you don't need to have everything figured out. Just bring yourself, an open mind and a little curiosity.

What did you learn about feelings when you were little?

  • Were you encouraged to cry when you felt sad?
  • Were you told to be brave, stop making a fuss or be a good girl or boy?
  • Were some feelings welcomed while others were brushed aside?

Perhaps you grew up in a home where emotions were openly expressed. Or perhaps they weren't talked about very much at all.

Whatever your experiences, they can shape how you understand feelings today. And when we become parents, we don't suddenly leave all those experiences behind.

Sometimes we find ourselves repeating familiar responses without even realising it. Other times, we're determined to do things differently but aren't quite sure what that looks like.

What if we started getting curious about those patterns before our babies arrive?

Let's explore this together

In this masterclass, we'll get curious about:

🌱

Your emotions

How you experience and express feelings, and what happens when things become overwhelming.

💌

The messages we carry

How our childhood experiences and the things we were taught about emotions can influence our responses today.

👶

Your baby's emotional world

How babies communicate their needs, experience connection and begin developing ways to manage feelings with the support of their caregivers.

🧭

Finding your own way

How greater awareness of your own emotional patterns can help you respond with curiosity, understanding and connection.

There is no such thing as a perfect parent, but there are conversations worth having before we become one..

Meet Steph — also known as Plum! 💜

Hello! I'm Steph. I'm a Family Wellbeing Consultant, Impact Speaker, former Specialist Public Health Nurse, home-educating mum of four boys and founder of Authentic Kids.

Through my professional work and my own experiences of motherhood, I've become increasingly curious about how the messages we receive as children can shape the way we see ourselves, experience emotions and relate to others.

I believe children come into the world already connected to so much — their bodies, feelings, senses, instincts and natural curiosity. And I want to help them stay connected to who they are as they grow. That means getting curious about ourselves as adults, too.

Oh, and why Plum?

My husband has called me Plum for years. Neither of us can remember how it started, but the name stuck! And now it's inspired The Plum Bunch, a parenting community I'm creating within Authentic Kids for mums and dads who want to explore parenting a little differently.
