Before They Arrive,
Let's Talk About You.

A different kind of preparation for parenthood.

I'm so pleased you're joining me!

There's so much preparation that goes into welcoming a baby. But how often do we stop to think about ourselves, the experiences we've had and what we might bring into parenting?

That's exactly what we're going to get curious about together.

We'll explore emotions, the messages we carry from childhood and how understanding ourselves can help us build meaningful connections with our children.
